Personal Workflow Assistant

Personal Workflow Assistant is the name of the Google OAuth application described on this site. It is a private, single-user tool operated by its account owner, with no public registration or shared service.

The assistant helps its owner organise chosen professional records and public information, prepare communications, run automated reviews, and send messages within the owner's instructions. It uses bounded Gmail searches to check relevant correspondence and prevent duplicate communications.

Google connection

The owner may authorise read-only Gmail access and permission to send email. The assistant cannot use these permissions before consent. The owner can pause the workflow or revoke Google access at any time.

Automated research and reviews use sanitised professional information through OpenRouter. Gmail correspondence is kept out of AI prompts. Private workflow records are separate from this public information site.

Privacy policy Terms of use

Read the privacy policy before connecting an account. For support, use the support contact shown on the application's Google consent screen.
